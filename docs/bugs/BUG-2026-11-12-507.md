## BUG-2026-11-12-507 — a blank job-card Due Date counted as overdue on a department tab; two writers ignored Auto-schedule OFF `production` `due-date` 🟡

🟡 **On branch** (`fix/hk0147-blank-due-date` → `main`). Ticket HK-0147.

**Symptom.** Ticket HK-0147 (Violet, 12 Nov 2026): clear the Due Date on every unsent job card, and leave it blank on new orders. Blank due dates were already possible (Planning → Production Lead Times → "Auto-schedule due dates" OFF, or clearing a date by hand), but two things did not handle blank:

1. The department-tab overdue count (`computeOverdueCounts`, `src/api/routes/production-orders.ts`) guarded `jc.dueDate IS NOT NULL` but not `''`. In text comparison `'' < '2026-11-12'` is true, so a cleared card counted as overdue.
2. Two job-card writers always wrote a date, even with Auto-schedule OFF: the stock-PO clone (`POST /api/production-orders/stock`, wrote `targetEndDate`) and the job-card sync from BOM (`src/api/routes/jobcard-sync.ts`, wrote `targetEndDate || startDate`). The SO-confirm builder (`_shared/production-builder.ts`, `blankDueDates`) already obeyed the switch.

**Fix.** The overdue query adds `AND jc.dueDate <> ''` (the same guard the edit-lock queries in `sales-orders.ts` / `consignment-orders.ts` already use). Both writers read `loadLeadTimeSettings` and write `''` when Auto-schedule is OFF.

**Data.** `scripts/hk0147-clear-unsent-due-dates-2026-11-12.sql`: preview, backup table, clear, after-check, restore. Run by the owner on prod.

**Regression.** `tests/blank-due-date-hk0147.test.mjs`.
