## BUG-2026-11-17-526 — Labor Cost Reconciliation left outsourced work in Overhead, the dashboard counted it as direct `employees` `labour` 🟡

🟡 **On branch** (`fix/labor-cost-outsource-direct` → `main`).

**Symptom.** The dashboard labour card's Direct labour and the Labor Cost page's "Production Labor (incl. EPF)"
differed in some finished months, with the same total. Measured on prod, read-only, 17 Nov: Aug 2026 card
RM 61,109.05 vs page RM 60,968.90 (RM 140.15); Sep 2026 RM 62,127.89 vs RM 62,072.45 (RM 55.44); Oct 2026
equal (RM 66,654.48). The page's Overhead line was higher by the same amounts.

**Cause.** The owner's rule (2026-11-06, work tracker items 79 and 83) is that outsourced work is direct labour
wherever it was keyed. The dashboard split (`src/lib/labour-to-date.ts`) follows it. The Reconciliation on the
Labor Cost page sorted each hour by the department it was keyed under, so OSC-001's hours keyed under R&D
(14.83 h in Aug, 5.87 h in Sep, none in Oct) stayed in Overhead. The page also tested "outsource" with its own
flag, not the server's OSC / OUTSOURCE test.

**Fix.** `src/lib/labour-to-date.ts` now holds `isOutsourceWorker` (used by the server loader and the page) and
`sumOutsourceOffProduction`. The page's hour buckets carry each bucket's outsourced cost; the Reconciliation
moves the part keyed outside production from Warehousing / Shortfall / Overhead to Production Labor, and files
an outsourced person's EPF, allowances (piece wages) and pay-vs-cost differences under Production whatever
their home department, the same as the dashboard. Overhead stays the closing figure, so the total and the
"Reconciled" badge do not change. The top cards (Production Hours Value, Remain) and the department table are
hours-based and stay as they were.

**Effect.** Expected after deploy: the Reconciliation's Production Labor equals the dashboard's Direct labour to
the sen (Aug RM 61,109.05, Sep RM 62,127.89, Oct RM 66,654.48). UNMEASURED until deployed.

**Class.** C33 row 9.

**Test.** `tests/labour-to-date.test.mjs`: one outsource test for server and page; outsourced cost keyed outside
production is summed by the line it leaves, production rows ignored.
