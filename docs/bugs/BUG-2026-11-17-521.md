## BUG-2026-11-17-521 — Screens split a shared card by piece scans, the payslip by PIC slot `efficiency` `worker-portal` 🟡

🟡 **On branch** (`fix/jc-finish-date-myt-eff-decimal` → `main`). HK-0124 same-term cross-check.

**Symptom.** On the worker phone the History days did not add up to the Home total, and the
same worker had a different efficiency on Department Performance, the dashboard and Floor Live
than on the payslip. Measured on prod, read-only, October 2026: 22 of 36 workers' by-piece
minutes differed from their payslip minutes. THI THI AYE (Fabric Cutting) showed 9,004 min
by piece against 15,444 min paid; EI PHOO WEI 25,413 against 19,525.

**Cause.** The payslip (`efficiency-allowance.ts`) splits a finished card between its two PIC
slots, half each when both are filled. Four other copies walked the piece scans instead
(`workforce-perf.ts`, which the dashboard, Floor Live, the KPI and the daily email use;
`department-performance.ts`; phone History and the leader app in `worker.ts`), and the email's
own fallback split by every distinct worker. The phone History also left approved extra time
off its days. The October gap came mostly from 183 Fabric Cutting cards where the office added
THI THI AYE to PIC 2 after the card was finished (142 of them on 2 Nov); the office add changes
the card's slots but not the piece rows.

**Fix.** One function, `creditCardToPics` (`src/lib/job-card-minutes.ts`): the payslip's split.
All five copies call it. Phone History puts each day's approved extra time on that day. Dashboard
feed cache bumped to `protoV` v10. Pay is unchanged.

**Prod check.** October 2026, read-only: the new rule gives every worker the same minutes as the
payslip (37 workers, 0 differ, 438,670 min both). Standard and production minutes were equal on
every October card, so only the split moved.

**Not fixed here.** Whether an office-added PIC with no scans should be paid is the owner's
call. The labour cost posted to the books (`po-cost-cascade.ts`) still splits by worker its own
way. The PIC change history does not record who made the change.

**Class.** C33 rows 2 and 4.

**Test.** `tests/credit-card-to-pics.test.mjs`.
