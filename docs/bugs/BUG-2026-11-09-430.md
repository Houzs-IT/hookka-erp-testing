## BUG-2026-11-09-430 — Report toolbar: the pressed Forecast and Edit Layout buttons read white on white `finance` `ui` 🔵

🔵 **Fixed on branch `feat/cashflow-inputs-all-accounts`; not yet verified on production.**

**Symptom.** Owner's screenshot, 2026-11-09, Cash Flow with the forecast on: between DETAIL L1 L2 L3 and "Capex & financing" the Forecast toggle was an empty white box with a grey border; its label could not be seen. Pressed, the Edit Layout toggle ("Done") does the same.

**Root cause.** `src/components/finance/report-shell.tsx` built both toggles as `${CTRL} px-3 ${on ? "border-[…] bg-[…] text-white …" : …}`. `CTRL` carries its own `border-[#D9D3CA] bg-white text-[#1F1D1B]`, so a pressed toggle had two background colours, two text colours and two border colours on one element. Which one shows is decided by the order of the rules in the compiled stylesheet, not by the order of the classes. Measured in the built CSS: `.bg-white` comes after `.bg-[#C0392B]` and `.bg-[#1F1D1B]`, `.text-white` after `.text-[#1F1D1B]`, `.border-[#D9D3CA]` after both pressed borders. So the result was a white background, white text and the resting grey border. The download icon had the same kind of clash (`text-[#4B5563]` beside CTRL's `text-[#1F1D1B]`); there the grey wins by the same ordering, so nothing showed.

**Fix.** `CTRL` is split into `CTRL_SHAPE` (size, radius, border width, font) and `CTRL_IDLE` (the resting colours); `CTRL` = both, for the controls that never change colour. The two toggles take the shape and one colour set: the pressed colours, or `CTRL_IDLE`. The download icon takes the shape and its own colours. Nothing changes for a control at rest.

**Regression.** `tests/finance-v2-report-shell.test.mjs`: no line that uses `${CTRL}` adds a background, text or border colour of its own; the two toggles' classes are pinned in their one-set-at-a-time form; the icon's grey is its only text colour.
