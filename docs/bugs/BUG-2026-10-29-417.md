## BUG-2026-10-29-417: Consignment note converted twice, billed returned items, priced other orders' lines at 0 `consignment-notes` `invoices` 🟢

🟢 **On branch** (`fix/cn-convert-guards` → `main`). Found in a review of past ticket fixes, 2026-10-29. Prod check (GET /api/consignment-notes, 29 Oct): 0 of 28 CNs converted, so no data to repair.

**Symptom.** (1) Two simultaneous "Transfer to Sales Invoice" requests on one CN made two invoices and added the total to `customers.outstandingSen` twice. (2) A fully returned line (status RETURNED, qty kept) was still billed, and a RETURNED or CLOSED note could still be converted. (3) A CN spanning several consignment orders priced the other orders' lines at 0. (4) `/return` on an already invoiced CN cut A/R by the returned value while the invoice still billed it, and a later void took the full total off A/R again.

**Cause.** (1) The only guard was a plain read of `convertedInvoiceId`; the `UPDATE` had no `convertedInvoiceId IS NULL` check. (2) Lines came from every CN item, with no status check on item or note. (3) Convert looked up prices only on the header CO; the CN list value (`cn-value.ts`) uses each item's own CO.

**Fix.** `consignment-notes.ts`: the convert batch starts with a row-locked guard (`FOR UPDATE`, failing `CAST`, same trick as `consignment-note-shared.ts`) mapped to 409; RETURNED lines are skipped (a partial return already lowers qty) and the A/R bump uses the billed total; RETURNED/CLOSED notes are refused with 409; prices come from `loadCoLinePriceIndex`/`priceForCnItem`, so list and invoice agree. `/return` is refused (409) on a CN with `convertedInvoiceId` (void the invoice first), with the same guard in its batch; its A/R reversal is removed. `note.tsx`: the menu item is disabled for RETURNED/CLOSED.

**Checked.** Unit tests; tsc clean.

**Regression.** `tests/cn-convert-guards.test.mjs`.
