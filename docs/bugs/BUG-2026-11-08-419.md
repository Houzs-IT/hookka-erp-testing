## BUG-2026-11-08-419 — Finance Dashboard Cash Flow card: Investing and Financing tabs always 0 `finance` `dashboard` ✅

✅ **Shipped and verified on production 2026-11-08** (#1201, then #1202 for the snapshot key). Read only: `/dashboard` months May–November showed the expected Investing, Financing, Free cash flow and Money in / out, and in + out = Net change in every month.

**Symptom.** On `/finance-dashboard`, the Cash Flow card's Investing and Financing tabs showed 0 for every month, while the Cash Flow statement for the same months showed capex and loan / deposit / finance-cost money. Measured on production 2026-11-08, read only. The other figures on the card tied to the statement every month: Net change = Cash Surplus = bank c/f − b/f, Operating = Net operation surplus, Money in − Money out = Net change.

**Root cause.** `GET /api/accounting/dashboard` read the card off the statement with `kind === "subtotal" && section === "CAPEX"` (and the same for LOAN / DEPOSIT / FINANCE_COST). The statement emits a subtotal row only for Revenue Collection; every other section is a depth-1 `group` row over its lines. The predicate matched nothing and the sum came back 0, a figure that reads as measured and is not (BUG-CLASSES C15, row 47).

**Fix.** `src/lib/cashflow-card.ts` (pure) sums investing and financing from the statement's `line` rows by section, which does not depend on how the rows are grouped. The same change carries three owner decisions of 2026-11-08:
- Financing includes Trade Finance.
- Free cash flow = operating − capex. It was operating alone, from an earlier "no fixed assets" rule.
- Money in / out net each entry's bank and cash legs first, so a move between our own accounts (a petty-cash top-up) is neither money in nor money out. Net change is unchanged.

Unallocated stays outside the three tabs.

**Follow-up (`fix/dashboard-cf-cache-bump`).** The first ship left `DASH_PAYLOAD_V` at `v20`. The `/dashboard` snapshot revalidates only when a source table changes, so production kept serving the old figures after the deploy. The key is now `v21` (BUG-CLASSES C22, a cache that a code change does not reach). The test guards the bump.

**Regression.** `tests/cashflow-card.test.mjs`: behaviour of the three functions, plus a source guard that the dashboard reads through them and never through a CAPEX subtotal.

**Not fixed here (owner's entry).** Petty-cash reimbursements are recorded as fund transfers into Cash in Hand, and almost no spending from Cash in Hand is recorded, so those expenses do not reach the statement by purpose.
