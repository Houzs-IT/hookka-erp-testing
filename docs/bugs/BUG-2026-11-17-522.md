## BUG-2026-11-17-522 — Screens rounded each half of a shared card, so a month ran up to 209 min over the payslip `efficiency` `worker-portal` 🟡

🟡 **On branch** (`fix/credit-half-minutes` → `main`). Follow-up to BUG-2026-11-17-521 (HK-0124).

**Symptom.** After #1417 the live Department Performance for October matched the payslip split,
but not to the minute. Measured on prod, read-only, 17 Nov: YE LI SOE showed 17,782 min against
17,574 on the payslip (+208), CHIT KO KO AUNG +204, most others under 25.

**Cause.** A card shared by two PICs gives each half its minutes. Four places rounded each half to a
whole minute before adding it up (`workforce-perf.ts`, `department-performance.ts`, and the phone
History and leader app in `worker.ts`), so a 113 min card counted 57 + 57. The payslip keeps 56.5.
YE LI SOE had 417 shared cards with an odd total in October, which is 209 rounded-up half minutes.

**Fix.** The halves are kept. Every card's minutes are whole numbers on prod (72,064 finished cards,
none with a fraction), so a share is always a whole or a half and sums exactly. `formatHours` rounds
to the minute when it prints "Xh Ym". Dashboard feed cache bumped to `protoV` v11.

**Class.** C33 row 2 (same figure, rounding drift).

**Test.** `tests/credit-card-to-pics.test.mjs`: two 113 min cards give each PIC 113, not 114.
