## BUG-2026-11-13-508 — Board report: Refresh showed the old copy, and "All labour" counted non-production time twice `board-report` `labour` 🟢

🟢 **Fixed on main** (#1391, #1392, then `fix/board-labour-split-nonprod`).

**Symptom.** Owner, 13 Nov 2026: the Board report's figures differed from live and Refresh did nothing. October's cover said Net profit RM 136,673 (29.5%); the live Monthly P&L said RM 93,618. Page 3 said All labour RM 80,178 while October payroll was RM 73,280 (「10月的人工total 70k+ 没有接近80k」).

**Root cause.**
1. Refresh starts a background rebuild; the page then polls without `rebuild=1`, and the server answered that poll with the PREVIOUS cached copy (`src/api/routes/reports.ts`, `/board`), so the page showed stale figures. October's copy had been built before part of the month's expenses were posted.
2. Page 3 added the non-production rows (production staff's clocked time in R&D / Warehousing / Repair — already inside their home-department payslips) on top of the production total.

**Fix.**
1. Polls send `&poll=1`; while the build's lock is fresh the server answers "building" (#1391).
2. All labour (payroll) = the payslip total (#1392); then the non-production time is split OUT of direct labour on its own rows — Direct labour (production) = payroll − non-production time (October: RM 73,279 − 6,899 = RM 66,380), every column filled (`src/pages/board-report/pages-finance.tsx`).

**Verified.** October rebuilt on prod: Net RM 93,618, equal to the Monthly P&L. Department labour checked against October payslips: gross by home department + employer statutory = RM 72,905 + 375 = RM 73,280.

**Regression.** `tests/board-report-background.test.mjs` (poll guard), `tests/board-report-review-1110.test.mjs` (labour split).
