## BUG-2026-11-17-520 — Board report: raw worker ids in "Lowest efficiency", headcount counted the whole worker master `board-report` `operations-report` 🟡

🟡 **On branch** (`feat/board-report-owner-review-1115` → `main`). Owner's marked-up review of the
October 2026 board report (2026-11-15), page 11.

**Symptom.** "Lowest efficiency" listed `worker-2e73bcfa`-style ids instead of names, and
"Headcount (production payroll)" read 43 when fewer production workers were on the floor.

**Cause.** (a) `collectWorkforce` (`src/api/lib/operations-report.ts`) named the ranked workers
from ACTIVE, non-TEST workers only and fell back to the raw id, so a worker who had resigned (or
had a blank name) but still had efficiency for the month printed as his id. (b) The tile read the
Financial Dashboard's `labourBase.headcount`, which counts every row of the worker master that
was employed during the month (office and non-production staff included).

**Fix.** (a) Names come from every worker; a blank name falls back to the employee number,
never the id ("Unknown worker" if the worker row is gone); TEST accounts never rank. (b) A new
board-report source counts distinct workers with a payslip under a production department
(`departments.is_production` ≠ 0) in the month — workers with hours clocked in one, for a month
with no payslips yet (`productionHeadcount`, `src/lib/board-report.ts`). The dashboard's own
headcount is unchanged. Tile renamed "Production workers paid".

**Test.** `tests/board-report-review-1115.test.mjs` — "10a" and "10b".
