## BUG-2026-11-10-496 — Leads header showed Open pipeline and Confirmed 100 times too small `sales` `leads` 🟡

🟡 **On branch** (`feat/form-safety-sweep` → `main`). Found while sweeping loading figures (review pass 2 C1-11 follow-up, WORK-TRACKER 128).

**Symptom.** Sales Leads, header right: "Open pipeline" and "Confirmed" read RM 10.00 for leads worth RM 1,000 in total. The list's own Est. value column showed the right amounts. A lead's catalogue line showed its target price the same way (RM 1,500 as RM 15.00).

**Cause.** `src/pages/leads/index.tsx` passed `openValueSen / 100`, `wonValueSen / 100` and the catalogue line's `price_sen / 100` to `formatCurrency`, which already takes sen and divides by 100 itself (`src/lib/utils.ts`). Display only: `est_value_sen` and `lead_products.price_sen` are stored in sen correctly (`src/api/routes/sales-leads.ts` rounds RM × 100). A search of `src` for `formatCurrency(…sen… / 100)` found no other instance.

**Fix.** All three go to `formatCurrency` in sen, and the two header figures show "—" until the leads have loaded. Regression: `tests/form-safety-sweep.test.mjs` ("BUG-2026-11-10-496 …").
