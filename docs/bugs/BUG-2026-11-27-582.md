## BUG-2026-11-27-582 — AI Connector: propose_email could address any outside recipient `mcp` `mail-center` 🔴

> **Last verified: 2026-11-27**

- **Report:** Weekly ERP R&D report 2026-11-27, finding #2.
- **Cause:** `confirm_action` runs on the same token as the propose call, so the AI can confirm its own email. Text planted in ERP data could steer it to mail data out (only while the owner's AI actions switch is on; off by default).
- **Fix (interim):** `buildEmailProposal` (`src/api/lib/mcp-actions.ts`) allows only the sending mailbox's domain or the person's own login address. The full fix (the person approves in the ERP UI) is the owner's call. Meeting guests are not covered yet.
- **Regression test:** `tests/mcp-actions.test.mjs`.
