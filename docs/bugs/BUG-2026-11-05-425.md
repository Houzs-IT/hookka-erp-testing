## BUG-2026-11-05-425 — October payroll saved 27 of 39 payslips and was approved with 12 missing `payroll` `api` 🟡

🟡 **Fixed on branch** (`fix/payslip-generate-all-or-nothing`, to `main`).

**Symptom.** EMP-038 resigned on 7 Oct, worked 6 days in October and got no October payslip. He was not alone: October 2026 on prod holds 27 payslips, against 35 for September. Twelve people paid in September have none: EMP-016, 019, 020, 023, 024, 025, 030, 031, 032, 033 (all still ACTIVE), EMP-038 and OSC-001 (both resigned in October). The 27 were approved, and Generate and Regenerate both refuse an approved month, so there is no way in the app to add the missing 12.

**Evidence (prod, read-only, 2026-11-05).** The 27 October rows were created one about every 0.92 s, from 08:21:12 to 08:21:35 UTC on 4 Nov, then nothing; all 27 were approved at 08:21:53. Every earlier month saved 31 to 36 rows in 2 to 4 s. Other resignees in October (EMP-017, 021, 041) did get payslips, so the "resigned workers are paid for their last month" scope was not the cause. `pg_stat_statements` (since 1 Sep) shows the payslip INSERT never above 9 ms and the next-id lookup never above 3 ms, so the time was spent between Cloudflare and the database, not in it.

**Root cause.** `POST /api/payslips` saved each payslip with its own statements as the loop went (id lookup, INSERT, read back). Nothing wrapped the run, so when the request ended part-way the rows already written stayed. `src/lib/api-client.ts` aborts every `/api/*` call at 30 s, and 27 workers at 0.92 s each plus the setup queries is about 30 s. The page then only showed "Error generating payslips", the refreshed list showed 27 rows, and `PUT /api/payslips` approved them because it never checked that every worker had one. Unmeasured: the request log for that exact call (the health page's 24-hour view starts an hour after it, the 7-day view is capped at 50 rows) and why each step was slow that afternoon.

**Fix.** `src/api/routes/payslips.ts`:
- Generate computes every payslip first, numbers the ids in memory from one lookup, and writes them in one `batch()` (one transaction) together with the read-back. A request cut off part-way now saves nothing. The per-worker round trips drop from three to one.
- Regenerate's DELETE of the old drafts runs in that same transaction, so a failure keeps the old drafts instead of leaving the month empty. Numbering restarts at 001 because every row of the period is deleted first.
- Approve (`PUT`, any status but DRAFT) refuses with 409 and `missingPayslips` when a worker that generate would pay (ACTIVE, or RESIGNED in that month, joined by the month end, not TEST) has no payslip for the period. Checked read-only on prod: September lists none, October lists exactly the 12 above. The check runs through `batch()` so it reads inside a transaction: Hyperdrive cannot hand back the cached "missing" list right after the operator regenerates.
- Ids still start from the next-id lookup on a first generate. A period string that is not YYYY-MM makes `nextPayslipId` fall back to the current month's prefix, where other rows can already exist.

Not changed: the 30 s client timeout, and the 12 missing October payslips, which need an owner decision (un-approve and regenerate recomputes all 39 with today's worker settings, and payment vouchers were raised on 4 Nov).

**Regression.** `tests/payslip-generate-all-or-nothing.test.mjs` drives the route against an in-memory database: a failure on the 3rd save leaves no rows; a regenerate that fails keeps the old drafts; approve refuses a month missing a resigned worker's payslip and allows a complete one. On `origin/main` 3 of its 5 cases fail; on this branch all 5 pass.
