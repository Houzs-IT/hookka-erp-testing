## BUG-2026-11-20-539 — Phone "Email to Supplier" on a PO fails every time `procurement` `mobile` `mail-center` 🟡
> **Last verified: 2026-11-20** against `src/pages/m/screens/procurement/PoDetail.tsx`, `src/pages/m/screens/procurement/actions.ts` (`poEmailDraft`), `src/api/routes/purchase-orders.ts` (`/:id/mark-emailed`) and `tests/po-mobile-email-route.test.mjs` (branch `fix/hk0113-mobile-po-email`, to `main`).

🟡 **On branch** (`fix/hk0113-mobile-po-email` → `main`).

**Symptom.** On the phone PO screen, More → "Email to Supplier" shows an error and nothing reaches the supplier (HK-0113, Mail Center go-live).

**Root cause.** PR #1119 (HK-0113, 26 Oct) moved desktop "Email to Supplier" to the Mail Center composer and deleted `POST /api/purchase-orders/:id/email`. The redesigned phone screen (`58ef6227f`, 11 Nov) was written against the old route and still POSTs to it.

**Fix.** The phone action builds the same draft as desktop (`buildPoEmailDraft`, PO PDF attached) and opens the Mail Center `ComposeDialog`; after the send it calls `POST /:id/mark-emailed`, so the mail goes from the operator's own mailbox and shows in their Sent folder.

**Regression.** `tests/po-mobile-email-route.test.mjs`: every `${url}/<action>` the phone PO screen calls is a real `/:id/<action>` route on `purchase-orders.ts`. Red on the old screen.

**Checked.** Unit test, typecheck. UNMEASURED: a real send from a phone (preview deploys have no mail provider).
