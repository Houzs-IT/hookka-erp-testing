## BUG-2026-11-24-552 — Scanned PI lines were always saved in the PO line's unit `procurement` `scan` 🟡

- **Symptom:** a supplier bills in another unit than our PO (PO in ROLL or BUNDLE, invoice in MTR or KG). Scanning the invoice saved every line in the PO unit, so the PO had to be amended and the invoice scanned again (Hub HK-0169, 2026-11-21).
- **Cause:** the desktop scan wizard (`src/components/scan-supplier-modal.tsx`) showed a UoM box but its PI POST body had no `uom`. The phone scan (`piDraftBody` in `src/pages/m/lib/scan-pi.ts`) did the same. The server then stored NULL, which means the PO line's unit. The PI form had per-line units since HK-0149 (#1372, #1382); the two scan paths were never given them.
- **Fix:** on a line linked to a PO line, both scans now show a Unit select and a "for __ <PO unit>" box (desktop: the PI form's `PiLineUomCell`). They send `uom` and `poQty`. The shared rule is `scanPiLineUnit` (`src/lib/scan-pi-line-uom.ts`): the scanned unit is kept when it is on the RM unit list, else the PO unit. Create stops while a line in another unit has no cover, or a cover of 0. The over-billing hints compare the cover, not the qty, with the PO's ordered qty.
- **Not changed:** the server, qty and price (no conversion), and GRN-linked lines, which still take the PO unit.
- **Regression tests:** `tests/hk-0169-scan-pi-uom.test.mjs`, `tests/hk-0169-phone-scan-pi-uom.test.mjs`, `tests/scan-over-billed-preflight.test.mjs`.
