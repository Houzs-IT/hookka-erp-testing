## BUG-2026-10-28-398: Invoice billed every piece at the first line's price when SO lines share product, size and fabric `invoices` `delivery` 🟢

🟢 **On branch** (`fix/hk0120-dup-line-invoice-price` → `main`). Reported 2026-10-28 by Siti (office, HK-0120): "can check why the price not same with invoice?" on SV-2610-035 (RX HOMICE SDN BHD).

**Symptom.** SV-2610-035 totals RM 1,950.00; its invoice INV-2610-103 (DO-2610-109, SENT, unpaid) totals RM 2,160.00. All 8 invoice lines read RM 270.00.

**Cause.** Measured on prod 2026-10-28, read-only. The order has six `SERVICE CHARGE ` lines, all size `-`, fabric `RANDOM`, at 270 ×2, 250 ×2, 220, 210, 250, 230. `priceForItem` (`src/api/lib/do-value.ts`) resolves a delivered piece through its PO's `(salesOrderId, productCode, sizeCode, fabricCode)`, and that map is first-one-wins, so every piece took line 1's RM 270. C21 recorded this map as "deliberate: for a PRICE any matching line's value will do"; that holds only while the matching lines agree on price.

Same shape across the book (prod, 2026-10-28): 10 orders carry lines that share the full key at different prices; 9 are invoiced, the delivered value off by RM 40 to RM 500 either way (SO-2608-221 under-billed RM 500). Only INV-2610-103 and INV-2610-066 are unpaid.

**Fix.** Tier 0 in `priceForItem`: `byPiece`, built by `buildPiecePrices`. A PO's `lineNo` is the builder's per-piece counter (`production-builder.ts` `poSequence`: lines in lineNo order, one PO per unit, one per SOFA line), so replaying it gives the line each piece came from. Used only when the PO's full key is contested (two or more prices) and the replayed line carries that same key; anything else falls through to the old tiers unchanged. The invoice, DO value, PO value and dashboard callers all go through `loadSoLinePriceIndex`, so they move together. `admin.ts`'s repair sweep builds its own index without `byPiece` and still reproduces what past invoices were priced at.

**Not fixed here.** Existing invoices are not re-priced. The two unpaid ones can be corrected by the office with Edit prices; the paid ones are Finance's call. `invoice_items.so_item_id` stays NULL on contested lines.

**Checked.** Replayed on prod rows (read-only) for all 10 orders: the new pricing equals each order's total (SV-2610-035 2,160 → 1,950; SO-2608-221 4,815 → 5,315). Unit tests; tsc clean; full suite 0 fail.

**Regression.** `tests/do-value-piece-price.test.mjs`.
