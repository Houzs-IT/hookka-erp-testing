## BUG-2026-11-17-532 — Department Revenue: Fabric Cutting booked only the first module of a sofa order `dashboard` `dept-revenue` 🟡

🟡 **On branch** (`fix/capacity-workers-punch-in` → `main`).

**Symptom.** Owner, 17 Nov: Department Revenue for 2026-11 showed Fabric Cutting RM 278,896 against Fabric Sewing
RM 397,412 — as if cutting could not keep up with sewing, which it does. Measured on prod (`GET
/api/dashboard/overview/revenue-split?period=2026-11`): the whole gap is sofa — Fabric Cutting sofa RM 83,914 (87
orders) vs Fabric Sewing sofa RM 195,170 (93 orders); bedframe is level (RM 183,312 vs RM 191,262).

**Cause.** Fabric is cut once per sofa order: one FAB_CUT job card on the order's first production order covers every
module (e.g. SO-2611-295-01's card is "5568-2A(LHF)+L(RHF)", qty 2). Measured on prod 17 Nov over all production
orders: 748 of 778 sofa orders have exactly one cutting card; 884 of 1,663 sofa production orders have no FAB_CUT card
while a sibling in the same order does. `loadDeptRevenue` books a department per production order that has cards in
it, so the -02 / -03 modules never booked under Fabric Cutting. Of the 214 sofa POs that finished sewing in November,
126 had no cutting card of their own. No other department shows the pattern (Fibre / Foam Cutting are missing on
older orders entirely — they are newer departments — not via a sibling).

**Fix.** `src/api/lib/dept-revenue.ts`: an `order_cut` CTE — when ALL FAB_CUT cards of an order (sales or
consignment) are done, every non-cancelled production order of that order without a FAB_CUT card of its own books
FAB_CUT on the last card's day. Display only (dashboard and Floor Live department figures); no posting changes.

**Test.** `tests/dept-revenue.test.mjs` — "Fabric Cutting: a module with no cutting card books when its order's one
cutting card is done".
