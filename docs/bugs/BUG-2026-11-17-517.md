## BUG-2026-11-17-517 — A job card finished before 08:00 Malaysia time saved yesterday's date `production` `timezone` 🟡

🟡 **On branch** (`fix/jc-finish-date-myt-eff-decimal` → `main`). Found during the HK-0124 same-term cross-check.

**Symptom.** A job card scanned or marked done between 00:00 and 08:00 Malaysia time got the
previous day as its finish date. A card finished on 1 Dec at 03:00 counted in November for
production revenue and the monthly efficiency allowance. Its own production order, finished in the
same scan, already got the Malaysia date, so the two could disagree. The office edit also capped a
typed finish date at the UTC day, so typing today's date before 08:00 saved yesterday.

**Cause.** The Worker runs in UTC. Six places cut the date from the UTC timestamp string:
`const nowIso = new Date().toISOString(); const today = nowIso.split("T")[0];`
(`production-orders.ts` QR scan, barcode scan, third scan path; `production-orders/_helpers.ts`
office status change; `lib/fg-completion.ts` fallback date; `import-completion/_shared.ts`, the
production order date when an import completes its last card). `tests/no-utc-today.test.mjs` only
caught the one-line form, and `scripts/check-clock-dates.mjs` follows `Date` variables, not the
ISO string, so neither saw it.

**Fix.** All six use `todayYmdMY()`. The saved instant (`completed_at`) was already right and is
unchanged. The other finish-date writes take a date an operator or a sheet supplied, so they are
not affected.

**Not fixed here.** Old rows keep their UTC date; correcting them needs a backfill from
`completed_at` and has not been asked for. Prod count of affected cards is UNMEASURED.
The same two-step UTC pattern is in 30 other `src/api` sites (invoice, DO, GRN, PO, SO, service
order, equipment, stock adjustment and R&D dates, among others). Not touched; the clock-date
detector should learn the ISO-string form before they are fixed in one sweep.

**Class.** None in `BUG-CLASSES.md`; the guard is `tests/no-utc-today.test.mjs`.

**Test.** `tests/no-utc-today.test.mjs`: "job card finish dates use the Malaysia day" pins the six sites (source text), and "a 03:00 Malaysia finish on 1 Dec is dated 1 Dec" checks `todayYmdMY()` with a mocked clock.
