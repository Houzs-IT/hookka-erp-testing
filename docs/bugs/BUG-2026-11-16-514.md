## BUG-2026-11-16-514 — Worker home showed announcements with no acknowledge button or state `worker-portal` `announcements` 🟡

🟡 **On branch** (`fix/hk0155-worker-ack-tag` → `main`). Hub ticket HK-0155 (Violet, 16 Nov).

**Symptom.** Violet's photo of EMP-001's worker home: "OVERDUE ORDERS - 3 orders" and other
order notices listed under Announcements, with no acknowledge button. Read as "the worker
received the notification but can't acknowledge it".

**Cause.** Not a lost ack. The only acknowledge control is the one-time "Got it" pop-up
(`src/pages/worker/index.tsx`, must-acknowledge modal). Once tapped, the notice stays on the home
list folded to its title, with nothing to say it was acknowledged. Measured on prod 16 Nov:
`GET /api/announcements/ann-89fc302e-acb/acks` → EMP-001 acked `2026-11-11T10:41:02Z`, 1 of 1.

**Fix.** Each home notice that the pop-up would not show (acked, and not reminded since) carries
an "Acknowledged" tag beside its category badge, in all four worker languages
(`home.announcementAcknowledged`). Same predicate as the pop-up, so a "Remind"ed notice loses
the tag and pops again. No list button: an un-acked notice always opens the modal, which covers
the list.

**Test.** `tests/worker-announcement-acked-tag.test.mjs` (fails on the pre-fix files).
