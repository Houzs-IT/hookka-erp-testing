## BUG-2026-11-11-501 — Efficiency read differently on the dashboard, the KPI, the daily email and the payslip `employees` `dashboard` `kpi` 🟡

🟡 **On branch** (`fix/efficiency-one-rule` → `main`).

**Symptom.** 11 Nov 2026, a read-only cross-check of every screen that shows production time or efficiency (code reading only, no database query). The same worker and month could show different efficiency figures, and two pages used the same words for opposite things:

1. Approved extra production time (an approved claim that a job took longer than its standard) counted on the payslip, Employees › Department Performance and the worker phone tile. It did not count on the dashboard Employees tab, the Department efficiency KPI, the board report, Floor Live or the Daily Efficiency email. A worker with an approved claim read lower there.
2. The dashboard Employees tab added earned minutes from days with no keyed production hours (today before hours are keyed, or any day whose hours are missing). The KPI card, whose comment says it "cannot disagree" with the page, skips those days.
3. The Daily Efficiency email (and the compliance "low efficiency" list and morning brief built from it) divided by hours in every department, warehouse and R&D included. Every other screen divides by production-department hours, so a worker who also helped outside production was listed as slow.
4. "Production time" meant hours keyed and "Prod hours" meant minutes earned on the dashboard. On the Employees page it was the other way round, and Department Performance called earned time "Production hrs".

**Cause.** One idea (earned standard minutes ÷ keyed production-department hours) was written out in several places, and each copy drifted. `buildPerfDays` (`src/api/lib/workforce-perf.ts`) had no extra-time input. The tile sums in `EmployeesDashboard.tsx` and its phone copy `emp-lib.ts` had no empty-day rule. `collectEfficiencyData` (`src/api/lib/efficiency-report.ts`) summed every `working_hour_entries` row.

**Fix.**
- `loadApprovedExtraTime` (`efficiency-allowance.ts`) reads approved `ADD_PROD` rows per day. `buildPerfDays` takes them as a required `approvedExtraTime` input, so each caller decides: the dashboard route (all time, through `section()` so a failed read marks the employee slice not live), `loadMonthPerfDays` (KPI and board report), Floor Live (one day). The dashboard feed cache key moved to `v9`.
- `hasKeyedHours` (`dashboard-shared-lib.ts`) is the one empty-day rule. `overallEfficiencyPct`, the desktop tile, worker rows, department table and "unlinked" row, and the phone copy all use it. Only the earned minutes of such a day are left out; its labour cost still counts.
- The daily email keeps `workingMinutes` (all departments, still printed and still used for present / absent) and adds `productionWorkingMinutes` for the ratio. Approved extra time is added to earned minutes. A worker with no production hours shows "—", and the compliance and morning-brief low-efficiency lists filter on production hours.
- Labels: "Hours worked" (keyed production-department hours) and "Earned time" (standard minutes earned) on the dashboard (desktop and phone) and the Employees page (Efficiency Overview, Employee Detail, Department Performance and their prints).

**Not changed.** The payslip still splits a shared card by PIC 1 / PIC 2, while the dashboard and Department Performance split by piece (a pay change, so it needs its own decision). The AI assistant's efficiency tools, the worker phone's daily rows and rounding differences are also unchanged.

**Impact.** UNMEASURED: no query was run, so how many workers have approved extra time, or how many days have earned minutes without keyed hours, is not known. KPI and board report figures are recomputed on every read, so closed months move too.

**Regression.** `tests/efficiency-one-rule.test.mjs` (fails on the old code). Class: C33 in `docs/BUG-CLASSES.md`, which also lists the copies still open.
