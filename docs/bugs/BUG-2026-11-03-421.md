## BUG-2026-11-03-421 — a 100-line DO could not be marked delivered; same-product lines all drew one FG batch `delivery` `cogs` 🟡

🟡 **Fixed on branch** (`fix/hk0128-big-do-deliver`, to `main`). Ticket HK-0128.

**Symptom.** Siti (office) could not mark DO-2610-119 (2990 HOME, SO-2610-427, 100 lines of SQUARE PILLOW RDM, one PO each) delivered; it stays LOADED. FE RUM shows 13 `PUT /api/delivery-orders/do-fd331e00` calls, every one aborted (status 0) at 30 s. The previous largest DO was 70 lines.

**Root cause.** The DELIVERED cascade sent one statement per line, one round-trip each: a product lookup and an `fg_batches` read per line, then 100 `fg_stock_events` INSERTs, 100 `fg_batches` UPDATEs, 100 `cost_ledger` INSERTs, 100 `invoice_items` INSERTs and 100 `delivery_order_items` draw-down UPDATEs, 511 batch statements in all. Past 30 s the global fetch wrapper (`src/lib/api-client.ts` `API_TIMEOUT_MS`) aborts, the Worker is cancelled and the transaction rolls back, so nothing lands.

Second defect on the same path: `consumeFGBatchesForDO` re-read `fg_batches` for every line, but its UPDATEs only run in the caller's batch, so every line of a same-product DO took the same oldest batch. This DO would have taken one 1-unit batch to -99; 608 prod batches are already negative (min -69) from earlier single-product DOs.

**Fix.** `consumeFGBatchesForDO` reads each product and its batches once and tracks remaining qty in memory (FIFO now walks the batches), then writes one set-based `UPDATE fg_batches ... FROM (VALUES ...)` and a multi-row `cost_ledger` INSERT. `buildFgStockEventStatements`, the auto-invoice `invoice_items` INSERT and `buildDrawdownStatements` are multi-row / set-based too (chunks of 200 rows). Same rows, same values.

Verified against prod with the real handler, reads in a read-only transaction and the batch run then rolled back: before 230 reads + 511 statements (about 24 s from KL), after 32 reads + 16 statements (about 3 s); 100 distinct batches each drop by 1, 100 ledger rows, 100 invoice lines all linked to their DO line, invoiced 100/100.

**Not fixed here.** The 608 already-negative `fg_batches` rows (FG stock value and past COGS). Needs a repair plan of its own.

**Regression.** `tests/do-partial-invoice.test.mjs`, `tests/fg-stock-events.test.mjs`, `tests/do-cancel-releases.test.mjs`, `tests/do-reverse-to-dispatched.test.mjs` read the multi-row shape.
