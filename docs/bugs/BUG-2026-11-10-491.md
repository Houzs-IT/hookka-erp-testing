## BUG-2026-11-10-491 — Trade finance Suppliers table: unticking a supplier with no Forecast P&L figure made its row vanish, with no way to tick it back `finance` `cash-flow` ✅

Found on prod 2026-11-10 while checking #1290 (read only, nothing saved). In Cash Flow → Forecast → Capex & financing, a trade-finance facility's Suppliers table lists a supplier when it has a Forecast P&L amount or is ticked. The facility's own suppliers are ticked by default, and none had a Forecast P&L figure yet. Unticking one dropped it from the list at once, so it could not be ticked again. After a Save it stayed gone until it got a figure.

Fix: `tfSupplierRows` takes `keep`, and the popup passes the facility's own suppliers (`paidSupplierIds`) plus the ticks it opened with. A kept supplier stays listed, unticked, with "-". Only a supplier never shown as kept, ticked or keyed is left out, as before. Nothing stored changes.

Regression: `tests/tf-supplier-ticks.test.mjs` ("a kept supplier stays listed unticked").

Superseded the same evening: the owner asked for a tick per supplier per month instead (WORK-TRACKER Cash Flow forecast item 22). The Suppliers table, `tfSupplierRows` and this regression test are gone; `tests/tf-forecast-ticks.test.mjs` covers the new rows.
