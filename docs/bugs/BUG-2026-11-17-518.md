## BUG-2026-11-17-518 — Avg Efficiency showed a fake ".0" and the printed card used a different average `employees` `efficiency` 🟡

🟡 **On branch** (`fix/jc-finish-date-myt-eff-decimal` → `main`). Found during the HK-0124 same-term cross-check.

**Symptom.** The Employees page printed Avg Efficiency as "84.0%" when the real figure was, say,
84.4%. The printed Efficiency Overview's "Avg Efficiency" card could also differ from the screen,
because it averaged each worker's own % while the screen divides total earned time by total hours.

**Cause.** `GET /api/department-performance` rounded every efficiency (totals, daily, per worker)
to a whole number, and the pages then printed it with `toFixed(1)`. The print card in
`employees.tsx` took a mean of per-worker ratios, so a worker with few hours weighed as much as a
full-time one.

**Fix.** The route returns one decimal (`Math.round(x * 1000) / 10`), like the worker phone. The
daily employee-state snapshot column is INTEGER; its writer (`employee-state-snapshot.ts`) already
rounds, so it keeps storing a whole number. The print card now uses total earned minutes ÷ total
production-dept minutes over its own rows: the same method as the header, though not the same rows
or date range, so the two numbers can still differ. The phone Department Performance tile prints
`toFixed(0)` and is unchanged.

**Class.** C33 row 7.

**Test.** `tests/efficiency-one-decimal.test.mjs`; the formula guards in
`tests/no-fabricated-*.test.mjs` and `tests/helper-screens-2b.test.mjs` now pin the one-decimal form.
