## BUG-2026-11-13-511 — Mail to two staff, or a Bcc, did not reach each person's Mail Center `mail-center` `inbound` 🟡

🟡 **On branch** (`fix/hk0150-cloudflare-inbound` → `main`). Hub ticket HK-0150; found while
preparing the switch of hookka.com mail to Cloudflare Email Routing (Mr Lim chose it, 13 Nov).

**Symptom (found in code, before the switch).**
1. A mail to siti@ with lim@ in Cc was stored once, under the first matching box; the other
   person (personal view) never saw it. Dedup was by Message-ID across the whole company, and
   each person's list shows only threads of their own box.
2. A mail Bcc'd to siti@ was filed under the first To: address, because the inbound payload
   carried only the To/Cc headers, not the box the copy was delivered to.
3. `mail-inbound-worker/wrangler.toml` pinned the Houzs Cloudflare account (816e…); the
   hookka.com zone is in account 27cd…, and Email Routing can only send to a Worker there.

**Fix.**
- The worker sends `deliveredTo` = the SMTP envelope recipient (Cloudflare calls it once per
  recipient); `mail-sync/sync.mjs` sends the IMAP box it read.
- `ingestInboundEmail` (`src/api/routes/mail-center.ts`) files the mail under `deliveredTo`
  when given, dedups by Message-ID **within that box**, and joins a reply only to a thread in
  the same box. Without `deliveredTo` the old first-@hookka.com-recipient rule stands.
- `wrangler.toml` account_id = 27cd35c9d93a9f81daa809d0b800b059.

**Not fixed here:** copies already stored before this change stay where they were filed.

**Regression test.** `tests/mail-inbound-delivered-to.test.mjs` runs the real ingest on an
in-memory SQLite database: two staff get a copy each and a repeat is deduped; a Bcc lands in
the delivered-to box; a reply joins its own box's thread; no `deliveredTo` keeps the old rule.
3 of its 5 tests fail on the old ingest code.
