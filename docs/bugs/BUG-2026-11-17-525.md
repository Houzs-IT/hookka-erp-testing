## BUG-2026-11-17-525 — Factory efficiency counted outsource and today's unlogged work over in-house hours `efficiency` `employees` 🟡

🟡 **On branch** (`fix/in-house-efficiency` → `main`). Found in the HK-0124 live check, 17 Nov.

**Symptom.** For November the Employees page header showed Avg Efficiency 124.1% and the printed
Efficiency Overview 114.0%. Measured on prod, read-only, 17 Nov afternoon.

**Cause.** Efficiency is earned standard minutes ÷ production-department hours. The header (and the
dashboard, KPI factory figure and the phone dashboard, which read the same day totals) added up every
finished card once a day, whoever it belonged to, while the hours were only the in-house people's. So
the top of the sum carried about 612 h that no logged hours stood behind:
- outsource cards, paid by piece with no hours: about 333 h;
- today's finished cards, whose hours only land at punch-out: about 209 h;
- cards with no PIC: about 42 h;
- in-house work on a day the person logged no hours: about 31 h.
The printed overview added up each person's month, so it left outsource out but still counted today.

**Fix (owner 2026-11-17).** Efficiency counts in-house people only: a person's earned minutes count on
a day only when they logged production hours that day. Applied where the day totals are built
(`buildPerfDays` in `workforce-perf.ts`, the daily rows in `department-performance.ts`), so every
screen that pools them follows. Department Performance's summary also gives each person
`workedDayProductionMinutes`, which the printed Efficiency Overview card adds up. Outsource keeps its
own report under Employees › Outsource. Dashboard feed cache bumped to `protoV` v12. Pay is unchanged.

**Effect.** November on live data, read-only, 17 Nov: header and print both 107.6% (3,988 h earned
on 3,706 h).

**Class.** C33 row 8.

**Test.** `tests/in-house-efficiency.test.mjs`: outsource and no-PIC cards are left out, work on a day
with no logged hours does not count, factory efficiency is in-house earned over in-house hours.
