## BUG-2026-11-26-561 — AI Connector "Allow" answered "Cross-site request" `mcp` `auth` 🟢

> **Last verified: 2026-11-26**

- **Symptom (reproduced live in Chrome):** pressing **Allow** on the AI Connector consent page (`POST /api/mcp/oauth/approve`) showed "Refused — Cross-site request", so nobody could finish connecting Claude / ChatGPT. Same bug found first on Houzs Connect ("Forbidden", owner report).
- **Cause:** the consent page was served with `Referrer-Policy: no-referrer`; for a form post the browser then sends `Origin: null`, and the handler refused any Origin other than the site's own.
- **Fix:** `src/api/routes/mcp.ts` — Referrer-Policy `same-origin` (still no referrer to other sites); `Origin: null` accepted only when `Sec-Fetch-Site` is `same-origin`/`none`; `same-site`/`cross-site` refused. The CSRF cookie check is unchanged. Same fix on Houzs Connect (houzs-connect #226).
- **Regression test:** `tests/mcp-consent-origin.test.mjs`.
