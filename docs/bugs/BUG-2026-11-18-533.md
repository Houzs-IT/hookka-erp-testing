## BUG-2026-11-18-533 — On the Day view the Labour cost card still showed the whole month `dashboard` `labour` 🟡

🟡 **On branch** (`fix/labour-card-day-view` → `main`). Left over from BUG-2026-11-17-524 (#1423, HK-0124).

**Symptom.** Dashboard › Employees with one day picked (31 Oct): the Labour tile showed RM 2,516.91
for the day, but the Labour cost card under it, labelled "Labour cost · 31 Oct 2026", still showed
RM 73,279.10, the whole of October. #1423 cut the card to the period, which fixed short ranges but
not the Day view.

**Cause.** A picked day is `period.day`, and `inPeriod` deliberately ignores it (charts keep the
whole month around the highlight). The dashboard builds its labour range from `inPeriod`, so on the
Day view `costByWorkerDay` covers the whole month and `labourMonthsWithin` cut the months to the
whole month. The card's revenue and full-time people (`periodTotals`) were whole-month too.

**Fix.** `EmployeesDashboard.tsx` cuts the card's worker-day costs to the focus (the picked day, else
the period) with `keepDaysWithin` before `labourMonthsWithin`, and `periodTotals` uses `inFocus`.
The tiles, charts and other periods are unchanged.

**History.** The earlier Day-view commit (78b6095bf, named in the HK-0124 tracker) is in no local or
remote ref, so this is a rebuild, not a cherry-pick.

**Class.** C33 row 6.

**Test.** `tests/labour-to-payroll.test.mjs`: a whole month loaded and cut to one day gives that day's
cost; the dashboard passes the focus-cut costs to the card and uses `inFocus` for its totals.

**Verify after deploy.** Dashboard › Employees, pick 31 Oct: the card total should equal the Labour
tile (RM 2,516.91 when measured 17 Nov). UNMEASURED until checked live.
