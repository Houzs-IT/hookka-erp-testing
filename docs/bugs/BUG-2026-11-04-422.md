## BUG-2026-11-04-422 — Department efficiency KPI: score did not equal the efficiency % `kpi` 🟡

🟡 **Fixed on branch** (`fix/hk0131-dept-efficiency-score`, to `main`). Ticket HK-0131 (Violet).

**Symptom.** The Department efficiency card for 2026-10 read "Measured: 90%" but "Score for this KPI: 80 out of 100". Violet wants 1% = 1 score: 50% scores 50, 79% scores 79.

**Root cause.** Not a code fault: `department_efficiency` (DEV-36) copied the `EFFICIENCY_BANDS` numbers of `production_efficiency`, the owner's 2026-08-07 rule of 80% scoring 60 and 75% or under scoring 0.

**Fix.** `department_efficiency` in `src/api/lib/kpi-catalog.ts` sets the floor, floor score and zero point to 0, which makes the bands one straight line: the score is the efficiency, capped at 100. The card's formula and step 5 say so. `production_efficiency` keeps the owner's bands. A LOCKED month is served from `kpi_periods`, so it keeps its settled score.

**Regression.** `tests/kpi-module.test.mjs` (department efficiency: the score is the efficiency, 1% = 1 point).
