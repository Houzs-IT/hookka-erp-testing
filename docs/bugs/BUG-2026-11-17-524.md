## BUG-2026-11-17-524 — The Labour cost card showed a whole month's payroll on a one-day view `dashboard` `labour` 🟡

🟡 **On branch** (`fix/labour-card-follows-period` → `main`). Found in the HK-0124 live check, 17 Nov.

**Symptom.** Dashboard › Employees with one day picked (31 Oct): the Labour tile showed RM 2,516.91
for the day, but the Labour cost card under it showed RM 73,279.10, the whole of October, labelled
"Labour cost · 31 Oct 2026". Its direct labour, non-production lines, departments and people were
all month figures too.

**Cause.** The tile adds up the payroll-scaled cost of each person's days in the period
(`use-labour-cost.ts`, fixed for short ranges in BUG-2026-11-11-503). The card (`LabourSplitCard.tsx`)
added up the whole `LabourMonth` rows behind the period, so any period shorter than a month showed the
month.

**Fix.** `labourMonthsWithin` (`src/lib/labour-to-payroll.ts`) cuts each month to the days shown:
each person keeps the cost of their own days, and the month's split, non-production lines and
departments shrink by the month's share. The dashboard passes the cut months to the card. A whole
month is unchanged.

**Limit.** Within the period, the split between direct, non-production and each department keeps the
month's proportions; it is not worked out day by day.

**Class.** C33 row 6.

**Test.** `tests/labour-to-payroll.test.mjs`: one day of October costs that day's RM, a whole month is
unchanged.
