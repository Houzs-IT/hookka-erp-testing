# BUG-2026-11-06-428 — Fabric usage card understated usage and showed old prices

**Found:** 2026-11-06, by the owner, on the Dashboard fabric cards.

October showed 2,274 m against 7,135 m on the orders actually cut, and Avg buy / Min-Max
never moved.

- The card read the `RM_ISSUE` ledger, which is only written when a batch can supply the
  metres. Of 704 bedframe and sofa orders finished in October, 242 had a fabric issue.
- No fabric has reached `rm_batches` since March 2026 and no GRN exists after June, so 120
  orders had no stock to issue from. Another 321 sat on fabrics with a long-fraction head
  batch or a duplicate item code. Those two causes are NOT fixed here and are not proven;
  the consume path still drops shortages.
- Avg buy and Min-Max read `RM_RECEIPT` for all time, so they stopped at March.

Fix: `src/lib/fabric-fifo.ts` and `src/api/lib/fabric-fifo-data.ts` take usage from the
Fabric Usage on completed FAB_CUT job cards and cost it first-in-first-out, with the latest
stock take lines as closing stock. Receipts are GRN batches, then invoices for goods with no
GRN. An OPENING batch above 1.5× the highest purchase price is left out (PC151-01: 12,030 m
at RM 35). `HOK-` and `FABRIC` code prefixes are folded onto one fabric.

Stock take is value-only: `stock_adjustments` holds no rows, so nothing deducts batches.

Regression test: `tests/fabric-fifo.test.mjs`.
