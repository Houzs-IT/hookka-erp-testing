## BUG-2026-10-26-392 — Overdue POs counted POs already billed in full from the PO `procurement` `purchase-orders` 🟢

🟢 **On branch** (`fix/hk0114-overdue-invoiced` → `main`). Reported 2026-10-26 by Siti (office, HK-0114) with a screenshot of the Purchase Orders cards: 108 Overdue POs (0 yellow · 11 orange · 97 red). She asked whether it is the list of purchase invoices that don't match their POs.

**Symptom.** The Overdue POs card (and its click-filter) on `/procurement` showed nearly every open PO as overdue, most of them red.

**Cause.** `isOverdue` in `src/pages/procurement/index.tsx` counts a PO past Expected Date unless it is RECEIVED / CLOSED / CANCELLED, and only a posted GRN moves a PO there (`poStatusStatements` in `grn.ts`). The office raises the purchase invoice straight from the PO (PI → From PO), which by design touches neither `receivedQty` nor status (`checkPoRemaining` in `purchase-invoices.ts` only caps the bill). Measured on prod 2026-10-26, read-only: 293 POs = 203 CONFIRMED / 37 CLOSED / 53 CANCELLED, zero RECEIVED or PARTIAL_RECEIVED; the only 37 GRNs are the one-shot `GRN-IMPORT-PI-*` legacy import. Of the 108 overdue, 101 had a live PI: 95 billed in full, 6 part-billed (PO-2610-015, -011, PO-2608-058, -050, -026, PO-2607-005), 7 with no PI at all (PO-2610-029, PO-2608-065, -035, -020, -016, -015, PO-2606-014).

**Fix.** `/api/purchase-orders/stats` returns `deliveredInFull` per PO: every line covered by max(received, invoiced). Invoiced qty is the same read as the PI guard (line `po_id` or header `purchaseOrderId`, PO line via `po_item_id` or its GRN line), org-wide, CANCELLED and DRAFT bills left out; bucketed by material code, else PO line, as the guard does (`src/api/lib/po-delivered.ts`). The page's `isOverdue` skips those ids. PO status and stock are unchanged — no GRN is written.

**Not fixed here.** Without a GRN those goods never went through GRN stock-in; whether the office should start posting GRNs is the owner's call.

**Checked.** Unit tests on the bucket rule; prod count after merge is a read-only check (expected ≈ 13).

**Regression.** `tests/po-overdue-invoiced.test.mjs`; `tests/purchase-orders-pagination.test.mjs` updated for the `/stats` map shape.
