## BUG-2026-11-09-434 — A PO could not see a goods receipt that spanned several POs `procurement` `po` `grn` 🟠

Since 2026-08-04 one GRN can receive lines of several purchase orders; such a receipt carries no header `poId` and records the PO on each line (`grn_items.po_id`). Every PO-side lookup still read the header only (`grns.poId = ?`), so for a PO received through a multi-PO GRN:

- the PO detail listed no goods receipt;
- the "posted receipt locks supplier and lines" guard did not fire, so the PO's supplier and lines could be edited over stock and cost already posted;
- "mark received" was refused with "Create + post a GRN first";
- three-way match left the receipt out.

Fix: one shared condition `GRN_OF_PO_SQL` — header names the PO OR any line receives one of its lines — used by all four. Three-way match keeps only this PO's lines of a multi-PO receipt (plus legacy lines with no `po_id` on a GRN whose header names the PO). `grn_items(po_id)` gets an index. From the Houzs review list (owner 2026-11-08).

Regression: `tests/po-sees-multi-po-grn.test.mjs`.
