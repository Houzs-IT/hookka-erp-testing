## BUG-2026-11-16-516 — Dispatch email never sent when the DO has no driver phone `delivery-orders` `customer-notify` 🔴

🟡 **On branch** (`fix/hk0157-dispatch-email-driverid` → `main`). Hub ticket HK-0157 (Siti, 16 Nov):
Houzs stopped receiving the "Goods Dispatched" DO email after DO-2610-086.

**Symptom (measured on prod, 16 Nov, read-only).** The Mail Center outbox has no
"Goods Dispatched" email after 22 Oct 01:48 UTC, for any customer. Invoice emails still go out.
Every DO from DO-2610-087 on uses KL Transport Services or NETS with a blank driver phone;
every DO that was emailed used JIVA, which has a phone. DO-2610-076 (KL Transport, blank
phone) is the one earlier DO missing from the outbox.

**Cause.** `queueDoCustomerNotice` (`src/api/routes/delivery-orders/_helpers.ts`) fills the
email's Contact No. When the DO has no driver phone and no `three_pl_drivers` row matches the
driver name, it joined `drivers d ON d.id = o.providerId`. `delivery_orders` has no
`providerId` column (the provider id is in `driverId`), so the query threw, the whole notice
returned 500, and neither the backend trigger nor the browser trigger sent anything. Added
2026-06-27 (6d4838bc0); it only bit once DOs without a driver phone became the norm.

**Fix.** Join on `o.driverId`, and wrap the best-effort Contact No. lookups in try/catch so a
failed lookup sends the email without a Contact No.

**Not fixed here.** DOs dispatched while this was broken are now DELIVERED/INVOICED; the
dispatch notice only sends for LOADED/IN_TRANSIT DOs, so they will not be re-sent.

**Test.** `tests/customer-notify.test.mjs` — "dispatch notice: Contact No. fallback joins on
driverId and can't kill the notice (HK-0157)".
