## BUG-2026-11-16-513 — the Production Agent wrote due dates back onto cleared unsent job cards, ignoring Auto-schedule OFF `production` `due-date` 🟡

🟡 **On branch** (`fix/hk0147-agent-honours-auto-schedule` → `main`). Ticket HK-0147, reopened by Violet 16 Nov 2026 ("all due date still there"). Follows BUG-2026-11-12-507.

**Symptom.** The 12 Nov clear (`scripts/hk0147-clear-unsent-due-dates-2026-11-12.sql`) blanked 10,096 unsent open job cards at 09:10 UTC. By 16 Nov, 9,908 of them had a date again. `schedule_proposals` shows `AGENT_AUTO` applied 11,832 proposals onto blank cards since 12 Nov (1,853 on 12 Nov, 9,822 on 13 Nov). Auto-schedule was OFF the whole time (`GET /api/production-leadtimes`). Measured by the owner's SQL and read-only GETs on prod, 2026-11-16.

**Cause.** The Production Agent heartbeat (`src/api/routes/agent-heartbeat.ts`) generates a proposal for every WAITING card whose due date is blank, past, or different from the engine's, and with auto-approve ON drains them into `job_cards.dueDate`. The drain only touches unsent cards (the Sent-lock), which is exactly the set the ticket asked to keep blank. Neither `generateProposals` nor `applyPendingProposals` read `loadLeadTimeSettings`. BUG-507 fixed the three builders but missed this fourth writer.

**Fix.** Both functions in `src/api/lib/schedule-proposals.ts` return early when Auto-schedule is OFF. Generation still expires and purges old rows first, so the queued proposals age out. A human approving a specific proposal (`decideProposals`) is not gated: that is a deliberate per-card decision.

**Data.** Re-run the clear after deploy: `scripts/hk0147-clear-unsent-due-dates-2026-11-16.sql` (new backup table `hk0147_due_date_backup_2`).

**Regression.** `tests/blank-due-date-hk0147.test.mjs` ("Production Agent proposals honour the auto-schedule switch"). Fails on the old code.
