## BUG-2026-11-11-503 — Dashboard labour for a short date range carried the whole month's payroll `dashboard` `employees` `labour` 🟡

🟡 **On branch** (`fix/labour-short-range` → `main`).

**Symptom.** Found 2026-11-11 by a code-reading cross-check of every labour figure (no browser or database check). With a range shorter than a month picked, for example the "Last 7 Days" preset across a month end, the Dashboard › Employees labour tiles and Operations › Costing booked each person's whole month of payroll onto the days in the range. The phone dashboard uses the same hook.

**Cause.** `useLabourCost` (`src/pages/dashboards/use-labour-cost.ts`) fetched working-hour entries for the picked range only, but asked for whole-month payroll. `scaleLabourToPayroll` (`src/lib/labour-to-payroll.ts`) spreads each person's month over their logged hours, so with only a few days of hours in hand it put the whole month on those days.

**Fix.** The hook reads the hours for the whole months around the range (`wholeMonthsAround`), scales them to payroll, then keeps only the picked days (`keepDaysWithin`) for the cost maps and the hour entries it returns. A whole-month period gives the same figures as before.

**Impact.** UNMEASURED: not checked in a browser yet. Only ranges that do not cover whole months change.

**Regression.** `tests/labour-to-payroll.test.mjs`: a 28 Oct to 3 Nov range keeps 3 days of a 30-day month (600 of 6,000 sen), not all of it; the hook reads whole months before it scales.
