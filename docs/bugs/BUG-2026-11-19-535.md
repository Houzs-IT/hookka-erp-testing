## BUG-2026-11-19-535 — A shop-floor scan left no row in the job card history `production` `audit` 🟡

🟡 **On branch** (`fix/scan-history-events` → `main`). Found during the HK-0124 live checks.

**Symptom.** The job card history (`job_card_events`, read by `GET /api/job-cards/:id/events`)
held only office edits. On 19 Nov, 643 cards finished on 18–19 Nov had 51 completion events
between them, on 26 cards, all from one office account with source `ui`. The worker scans that
finished the other cards, and the scans before every office edit, were not recorded. So a
dispute such as the 183 Fabric Cutting cards where the office added a second PIC after the card
was done (HK-0124) could show the office change but not the scans before it.

**Cause.** Phase 6 (a102df9d5, 2026-04-24) wired the event log into the office PATCH only. Its
comment said the scan handlers "will wire its own 'scan' source later"; that never happened. The
`"scan"` source type existed with no writer.

**Fix.** The three scan handlers in `src/api/routes/production-orders.ts` (`/:id/scan-complete`,
`/:id/scan-complete-dept`, `/:id/scan-complete-shared`) now call `recordScanEvents` right after
their `UPDATE job_cards`. It diffs the card before and after the scan, the same diff the office
edit uses, and writes STATUS_CHANGED, COMPLETED_DATE_SET and PIC_ASSIGNED / PIC_CLEARED rows with
source `scan`, the worker's name as `actorName`, and the dashboard user as `actorUserId` when an
office scanner scans on a worker's behalf (null for the worker's own phone). The office path's
"batch, dead-letter to `audit_dlq` on failure" block moved into `recordJobCardEvents` in
`src/api/lib/job-card-events.ts` and both paths share it, so a failed event write never fails the
scan.

**Not fixed here.** Cards scanned before this ships keep no scan history; nothing exists to
rebuild it from. The Google Sheet webhook and the completion import still write no events (there
is no `sheet` source type). `reconcileCompletedAt` still compares the UTC day of `completed_at`,
so an office re-date of a card scanned before 08:00 Malaysia time drops its stored finish time;
the owner chose to leave that (2026-11-19), since no screen reads the time today. Extra writes on
prod per scan (about 1–3 event rows) are UNMEASURED.

**Test.** `tests/scan-job-card-events.test.mjs`: a completing scan writes the three events with
source `scan`; an office scanner is recorded as the actor user; a scan that changes nothing
writes nothing; a failed batch is dead-lettered, not thrown; and every `UPDATE job_cards` in the
scan handlers is followed by `recordScanEvents`.
