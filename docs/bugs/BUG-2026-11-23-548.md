## BUG-2026-11-23-548 — MRP counted fabric POs ordered in ROLL as metres `mrp` `procurement` 🟡

- **Symptom:** live MRP right after the phase-1 deploy (2026-11-23). BROWNIE-01 showed "PO outstanding 2", but the open line on PO-2611-062 is **2 ROLL**, while stock and the BOM count in MTR. The 2 rolls were netted as 2 metres, so the shortage was understated. Same for HG1313-01, BO315-21, NICCA-06 and others: 51 of the open fabric PO lines are in ROLL.
- **Cause:** the open-PO supply query ignored `purchase_order_items.unit`. The system has no ROLL→MTR conversion anywhere (none of the ROLL fabric lines has been received yet, so GRN never had to convert one either).
- **Fix:** only PO lines in the material's own unit are netted. Spelling variants are merged (METER = MTR, YARD = YRD). Lines in another unit are listed in amber under PO outstanding ("+2 ROLL") and in the expanded row ("Also on order in another unit — not counted, check before ordering more"), with a footer note. Converting rolls to metres needs the metres-per-roll from the owner; UOM is owner-controlled.
- **Also added:** `GET /api/mrp/consume-log?since=` returns a read-only audit of the per-WIP deduction marks and the RM_ISSUE lines on those orders.
- **Regression test:** `tests/mrp-plan.test.mjs`.
