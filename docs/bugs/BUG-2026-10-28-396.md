## BUG-2026-10-28-396 — Paid POs stay on Overdue POs and a CONFIRMED PO has no close path `procurement` `purchase-orders` 🟢

🟢 **On branch** (`fix/hk0119-po-short-close` → `main`). Reported 2026-10-28 by Siti (office, HK-0119) with a screenshot of the Purchase Orders cards: 27 Overdue POs (0 yellow · 5 orange · 22 red). She asked to remove the POs that are already paid.

**Symptom.** POs whose purchase invoice is PAID still count on the Overdue POs card, and the office has no button to take them off except Cancel.

**Cause.** Two things. (1) Payment never reaches the PO: `supplier_payments` links to a PI (`purchase_invoice_id`) only, and the card's rule (BUG-2026-10-26-392) counts a PO delivered only when every line's max(received, invoiced) covers the ordered qty. (2) A PO can be closed only from RECEIVED (`VALID_TRANSITIONS` in `purchase-orders.ts`), RECEIVED needs a posted GRN, and the office posts no GRN, so a CONFIRMED PO the supplier under-delivered can never close. Measured on prod 2026-10-28, read-only: 10 of the 27 carry a PAID PI. 5 billed short (PO-2608-050 105/135, PO-2608-026 180/400, PO-2607-020 9/20, PO-2607-007 164/165, PO-000111 13/15); 2 billed under another code (PO-2607-053 `NLY-D25-0.5"` billed as `NLY-D25-1"`, PO-2607-035 `NLY-D35-3"` as `NLY-D25-3"`); 3 legacy POs (PO-000107, -108, -110) whose code-less lines are billed in full by PI lines with no code and no PO line, matched only by name.

**Fix.** (A) CONFIRMED / PARTIAL_RECEIVED → CLOSED is now a legal transition. PO detail shows **Close PO** on those statuses; it reuses the Cancel-with-reason modal (min 10 chars) and appends `[CLOSED YYYY-MM-DD: <reason>]` to the notes. RECEIVED still needs a posted GRN. CLOSED was already terminal for every open-PO reader (Overdue card, `po-delivered.ts`, fabric-usage incoming, dashboard-overview, lock-helpers). (B) `poIdsDeliveredInFull` buckets a code-less PO line by its material name, and a code-less invoiced row that matched no code and no PO line falls back to that name. A coded invoiced row never matches by name.

**Not fixed here.** Whether the short-billed balance is still coming is the office's call, per PO; nothing is closed automatically. The two wrong-code PIs stay as billed.

**Checked.** Unit tests (below); tsc clean. Canary check on prod data is read-only after the PR opens: expected card 27 → 24 (the three legacy POs), the rest leave only when the office closes them.

**Regression.** `tests/po-short-close.test.mjs` (PUT through the real route), `tests/po-overdue-invoiced.test.mjs` (name fallback).
