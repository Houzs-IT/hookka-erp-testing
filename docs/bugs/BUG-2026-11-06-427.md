# BUG-2026-11-06-427 — Cash Flow tabs empty, saved forecast late on the dashboard

**Found:** 2026-11-06, by the owner, on the Finance dashboard.

## Cash Flow tabs

Investing and Financing read 0 in every month, Free Cash Flow only repeated
Operating, and the bars never changed between tabs.

- The cash-flow statement prints the sections below the operating result as
  `group` rows. The dashboard looked for `subtotal` rows, found none and summed
  to 0. The figures were always in the statement and in the Detail view.
- Free cash flow was hard-coded to operating, from when there were no fixed assets.
- Financing also left out Trade Finance and Unallocated, so the three tabs could
  not add up to Net change.
- The bars were always the bank totals.

Fix: `cashflowTabTotals` in `src/lib/cashflow-engine.ts` adds up the account
lines. Each section sits in exactly one tab, free cash flow = operating + investing,
and the bars show that tab's money in and out. Payload version v21.

## Forecast

`withSnapshot` serves the stored dashboard and rebuilds behind it, so the first
look after a save showed the old targets, and a dashboard left open in another
tab never re-read at all. `PUT /api/accounting/forecast` now drops the stored
copies, the save clears the client cache, and the page re-reads when its tab
becomes visible again.

Regression test: `tests/cashflow-dashboard-tabs.test.mjs`.
