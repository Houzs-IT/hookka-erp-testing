## BUG-2026-11-10-441 — Documents raised before 08:00 on the 1st got last month's number `procurement` `sales` `delivery` `production` `hr` 🟠

The Worker runs in UTC. Every document-number builder took its YYMM from `new Date().getFullYear()` / `.getMonth()`, i.e. the UTC month, so a document raised between 00:00 and 08:00 Malaysia time on the 1st of a month was numbered in the previous month (a PO at 07:30 on 1 December became `PO-2611-…`). The doc-number service's `utcYymm()` (payment vouchers and other configurable series) did the same. Found by the cross-system review (`docs/plans/2026-11-10-cross-system-review.md`, item P1); Houzs fixed theirs in Houzs-Century/Houzs-ERP#5464 and #5493.

Fix: `mytYymm()` in `src/lib/doc-number.ts` (Malaysia month, UTC+8) is used by every builder: PO, GRN, DO, SO / SV (when no order date is given), intercompany SO, packing lists (PL / CPL, both builders), consignment orders and notes, service orders and cases, SOH, QC inspections and pending, stock adjustments, payslips (fallback), R&D projects, and `doc-number-service` (vouchers). `utcYymm` stays, marked deprecated. Numbers that already exist are unchanged.

Not changed (not document numbers): the worker portal's "this month" period and a few agent / scheduling date helpers — left for a separate sweep.

Regression: `tests/doc-numbers-malaysia-month.test.mjs` (boundary cases + a source pin that no builder derives YYMM from the server month).
