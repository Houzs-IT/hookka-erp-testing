## BUG-2026-11-26-560 — Mail Center auto-sent log showed one-time sign-in links to ordinary users `mail-center` `security` 🟢

> **Last verified: 2026-11-26**

- **Symptom (code reading, not exercised on prod):** `GET /api/mail-center/outbox` (snippet) and `/outbox/:id` (full text + HTML) return auto-sent emails to anyone with a mailbox in scope — nearly everyone, via personal mailboxes. When a direct send fails, invite and password-reset emails land in `outbox_emails` with their live one-time links (`routes/auth.ts`, `routes/users.ts`), so a staff member could open a colleague's reset link.
- **Cause:** the outbox endpoints share the inbox's bar (`scope.isAdmin || scope.addresses.length`), while the stored bodies can carry credentials.
- **Fix:** `src/api/lib/mail-outbox-mask.ts` `maskLoginLinks` replaces `/reset-password|/accept-invite|/invite?token=…` and `/reset|/invite/<token>` links in the snippet and both bodies unless `scope.isAdmin`. Same helper shipped to Houzs ERP (#6068) and Conts (#1293). Found by the 2026-11-26 Mail Center parity review.
- **Regression test:** `tests/mail-outbox-mask.test.mjs`.
