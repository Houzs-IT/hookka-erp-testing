## BUG-2026-11-09-433 — A purchase invoice's auto stock-in GRN read "received, not billed" `procurement` `pi` `grn` 🟠

Confirming a purchase invoice receives its matched raw-material lines through a system GRN (`src/api/lib/pi-auto-grn.ts`, owner 2026-10-27). The GRN was never linked back: its lines kept `invoiced_qty = 0` and the PI lines no `grn_item_id`. So the GRN looked "received, not yet billed" and its goods could be pulled into a SECOND purchase invoice off the GRN — paying the supplier twice for one delivery.

Fix: in the same batch that writes the GRN, every GRN line's `invoiced_qty` is set to what the invoice billed on it, and a PI line that maps to exactly one GRN line points at it (`grn_item_id`), so voiding the invoice restores it through the normal path. A PI line split across several PO lines keeps no pointer (one column cannot name two GRN lines); its GRN lines stay marked billed. Applies to invoices confirmed from now on; earlier system GRNs are not backfilled. From the Houzs review list (owner 2026-11-08).

Regression: `tests/pi-auto-grn-link.test.mjs`.
