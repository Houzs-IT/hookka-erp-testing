## BUG-2026-11-18-534 — An outsource worker's My Pay showed RM 0.00 though piece wages were imported `worker` `my-pay` 🟡

🟡 **On branch** (`fix/hk0160-completed-so-no`). Tickets HK-0161 "Did not show amount?", HK-0162, HK-0163.

**Symptom.** An outsource worker (per-day pay mode, day rate 0, basic 0) opened My Pay and saw RM 0.00
for the month, while the office had imported piece records for them and the draft payslip carried the wage.

**Cause.** `GET /api/worker/payslips` builds the current month from the labour engine (attendance, OT,
allowances) and never read `outsource_piece_records`. The card also printed a "Monthly salary RM 0.00" line.
Stored months were no better: piece wages ride inside `allowances_sen`, so they showed as a bare "Allowance".

**Fix.** The endpoint syncs scanned orders into records, then returns `pieceWagesSen`, `pieceLines` and
`piecePending` on `current` (estimated gross includes them) and `pieceWagesSen` on each history month.
My Pay shows a "Piece wages (N)" line that taps open to one row per order, lifts piece wages out of
"Allowance" on stored months, hides the RM 0.00 salary line, and lists orders with no rate as "Rate not set".

**Class.** A figure shown on the phone that the payslip engine does not read (same family as the
payslip-vs-estimate drift rows in docs/BUG-CLASSES.md).

**Test.** `tests/outsource-piece-scan.test.mjs`: share math, and the sync's insert / skip-imported /
no-rate / two-outsource / undo / locked-month cases against a fake database.

**Verify after deploy.** On staging with an outsource account at 360 and 390 px: My Pay equals the draft
payslip's piece wages. UNMEASURED until checked live.
