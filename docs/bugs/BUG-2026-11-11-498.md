## BUG-2026-11-11-498 — Orders unticked at dispatch went SHIPPED while on no delivery order `delivery` `do-cascade` 🔴

🟡 **On branch** (`claude/fix-do-removed-order-unship-5364d0` → `main`).

**Symptom.** 10 Nov 2026 16:41 MYT, DO-2611-026 dispatched from the scan page with three orders unticked under "Adjust load". SO-2610-077, SO-2610-074 and SO-2610-189 went READY_TO_SHIP → SHIPPED ("DO dispatched") although they are on no delivery order and their pieces are still in Pending Delivery. Owner, 11 Nov 2026: an order taken off a DO at or after dispatch "Should go back" to Ready to Ship.

**Cause.** `applyDeliveryOrderUpdate` (`src/api/routes/delivery-orders/_helpers.ts`, shared by the office `PUT /api/delivery-orders/:id` and the QR `POST /api/public/do-qr/:token/advance`) only queues the item DELETE/INSERT in its batch. The DRAFT → LOADED stock step used the new items, but the SO → SHIPPED cascade called `resolveDoSalesOrderIds`, which reads `delivery_order_items` — still the OLD rows. Second hole: an item edit on a DO that had already left (LOADED / IN_TRANSIT / DELIVERED without a live invoice) never moved a removed order back.

**Fix.** `buildDoItemEditSoStatements` (same file) owns the SO side of the save: DRAFT → LOADED marks SHIPPED only the orders on the post-edit item set; an item edit on a DO that already left sends an order that lost its last piece SHIPPED → READY_TO_SHIP with a `so_status_changes` row ("Removed from DO"), unless another LOADED / IN_TRANSIT / DELIVERED / INVOICED DO still carries it (`soRidesOtherLiveDo`, now shared with the cancel reversal). DELIVERED / INVOICED / CLOSED orders are not touched. Regression: `tests/do-removed-order-unship.test.mjs`.

**Not fixed here (open).** (1) An item edit on a DO that already left does not release the removed production orders' `fg_units` (they stay `LOADED` with this `doId`, no STOCK_IN counter-movement), so a later dispatch on another DO skips them. (2) The LOADED → DRAFT un-dispatch reverts every SHIPPED order on the DO without checking whether another dispatched DO still carries it. (3) An item edit in the same save as → DELIVERED still builds the SO / invoice / COGS cascade from the old rows; no screen sends both today. The three prod orders are repaired by the owner, not by this change.
