## BUG-2026-10-24-391 — Financial Dashboard: the ratio axis read 0, seven-digit axis labels lost their first digit, the Balance Sheet drew an empty tab track `finance` `dashboard` 🟢

🟢 **On branch** (`fix/dashboard-axes-tabs` → `main`). Found 2026-10-24 while taking screenshots of `/finance-dashboard` (with made-up figures, on a local page) for the dashboard's UI spec the owner asked for; the owner then said 「可以，修」.

**Symptom.** Three display faults on the Financial Dashboard, the figures themselves right:

1. Financial Ratios: every y-axis tick read `0`.
2. Balance Sheet (and any card whose values reach seven digits — e.g. a quarter's revenue): a label such as `1,400,000` lost its first digit at the left edge.
3. Balance Sheet: an empty grey tab track beside the title.

**Cause.** One shared card (`ChartCard` in `src/pages/finance-dashboard.tsx`) assumed every chart is money and every card has tabs:

1. Its y-axis always used the money format `rm()` — sen divided by 100, no decimals — so a ratio of 39.05 printed `0`.
2. No axis set a width, so recharts kept its fixed 60 px, enough for `600,000` but not for `1,400,000`.
3. It always rendered the `SegmentedSwitch`, which draws its track even with no options.

**Fix.** `ChartCard` takes `yFormat` (money unless a card says otherwise); Financial Ratios passes `ratioTick` (`src/lib/finance-chart-look.ts`: `40%`, `1.5`, two decimals at most). Every left value axis on the page (`ChartCard`, the salary cards, Cost Structure) sets `width` from `yAxisWidthFor(values, format)` — the widest label, taken a quarter above the largest value because the axis rounds its top up, never under the old 60, so a six-digit axis is unchanged. The tab switch renders only when the card has tabs.

**Checked locally** (the real page on made-up figures): the Balance Sheet axis shows `1,400,000` whole, the ratio axis `0%`–`40%` (Current ratio `0`–`1.4`), no track beside Balance Sheet, the six-digit axes keep their width.

**Regression.** `tests/finance-dashboard-axes.test.mjs` (the width helper, the ratio tick, every left axis sized, the guarded tab switch).
